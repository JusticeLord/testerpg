# Prompts de conceito e revisão



Registra-se abaixo o conjunto de prompts para permitir revisão da intenção visual. As imagens foram produzidas pelo recurso integrado de geração de imagens; não foi utilizado CLI/API externo. Os prompts são especificações de conceito, não contratos de implementação. As imagens permanecem apresentadas separadamente desta documentação.


### 01_brumaflor — geração inicial

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Project XP Forge RPG Lab, first quest A Luz do Caminho. Create a polished original 2D pixel art concept sample for approval, NOT a final game-ready tileset. Consistent art bible: warm everyday medieval fantasy, welcoming craftsmanship, exploration, clear silhouettes and clustered pixels. Visually a modern handcrafted pixel RPG with rich volume and restrained detail: strictly crisp square pixel clusters, no smooth painted gradients, no 3D render, no blur, no photorealism, no fake retro noise. Orthographic top-down three-quarter view, camera approximately 50 degrees above ground, visible roofs and front/side facades, no horizon, no vanishing point, NOT a diamond-grid isometric game. Exaggerated readable forms, honest domestic materials, cream plaster, aged chestnut timber, muted burnt-clay roofs, deep moss green foliage, blue-slate shadows, restrained amber highlights. Upper-left daylight, darkest outlines selectively, modest contact shadows. Atmosphere inspired by the hospitality and exploration of a starting medieval fantasy village and the clarity of handcrafted farm RPG sprites, but completely original architecture, shapes, costumes, UI and geography. No recognizable franchise elements, logos, magical runes, factions, new NPCs, new regions, quests, currencies or mechanics. Adult-sized sprite characters with expressive readable faces and approximately four-head body proportions, not tiny stick figures. Asset type: landscape 16:9 gameplay-view environment sample titled only "VILA BRUMAFLOR" in a small clean heading and "CONCEITO • EM APROVAÇÃO". Show a compact but convincing village composition with a broad irregular dirt central square, five distinct modest domestic buildings, kitchen gardens, fences and lush deciduous trees, open paths with generous diagonal circulation. Blacksmith Dario's open-sided workshop with small stone forge chimney and anvil on left, Iria's modest herb garden and worktable on right, residential buildings behind, never a tavern clone or castle. A curving dirt path exits upper-right to the nearby forest. At the village-to-trail bend show exactly ONE service lantern: crooked dark metal housing, sooty glass and torn leather retaining strap, unlit, with a broken clasp. Scene contains exactly four small people: Mara (adult woman dark auburn tied hair, worn short ochre cape, sturdy brown work clothes, service cord), Dario (broad adult man dark short beard, blue-grey shirt, soot-stained leather apron), Iria (adult woman dark braided hair, pale linen clothing sage green apron, leaf basket) and generic protagonist (simple muted blue/teal travel tunic and trousers, practical boots, no specified class). Buildings and foliage substantial relative to sprites. Keep the traversable ground in center clearly readable, rich natural tile transitions, flowers only at edges. No lake, river, bridges, huge city, extra shops or extra inhabitants. This is one beautiful full-frame game-scene sample, not a collage, not a procedural placeholder.
```
